# GEO5016 Geomatics Supervisor Assessment Rubric

The internship report (70% of the mark) is assessed by the Geomatics supervisor, using the rubric below. Each item is scored on a scale from below 5 to 10.

Conclusions are clearly and concisely formulated in a logical order. Has identified the added value of the research and relates this to the goals of the internship organisation.</td><td>Has clearly linked conclusions to the goals of the research, and all conclusions are substantiated by results.
