# GEO5016 Internship Report Requirements

## Cover

The cover should contain the text: *Internship Report Geomatics for the Built Environment*

- Title, your name, and the month and year of finalization of the report

Further the cover should contain:

- TU Delft logo
- An appealing illustration related to your internship work

## First pages

- **First page:** figure caption of the cover illustration
- **Second page:** no text
- **Third page** should include the text:
  - Internship Report Geomatics for the Built Environment
  - Title of your report
  - Your name
  - Month and year of finalization of the report
  - TU Delft logo
  - Name of the Geomatics tutor
  - Name of the company
  - Name of the internship supervisor
  - This report forms the written record of an internship carried out in the period &lt;&lt;date of start&gt;&gt; - &lt;&lt;date of end&gt;&gt;. The internship is an elective of the MSc Geomatics for the Built Environment, Delft University of Technology, awarded with 10 ECTS.

## Body text

- **Abstract** (max 200 words)
- **Introduction** (max 1,000 words)
- **Description of the company** (main products, organisation) (max 250 words)
- **Description of the department of the company** (max 150 words)
- **Description of the internship topic** (context of research, background of the overall project and how your topic fits, problem statement, formulation of goals and tasks) (max 600 words)
- **Research methodology** (max 1,000 words)
  - Data used
  - Equipment/software used
  - Developed algorithms / models
  - Analysis methods/experiments
- **Results, analysis and reflection** (max 1,000 words)
- **Conclusions, discussion, and recommendations of your research** (max 1,000 words)
- **Reference list**
- **Personal reflection on your internship** (max 1,000 words): were your knowledge and skills sufficient? Which literature did you study to get acquainted with the project? What did you learn during the internship? Which topics would you suggest to include in the Geomatics programme?
