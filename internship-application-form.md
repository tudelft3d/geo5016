# GEO5016 Internship Application Form

(maximum 800 words)

The application form should contain:

- Your name, study number, email address and completed Geomatics core courses
- Short description of the company (max 1 paragraph)
- Name, affiliation and qualifications of the supervisor at the company/organization (your Workplace Supervisor)
- Name and affiliation of the proposed Geomatics tutor (your Geomatics Supervisor)
- Title / topic of the work you will carry out, expected achievements and deliverables to the company/organisation
- Motivation why you want to carry out this work and an indication of how it will contribute to your Geomatics knowledge and skills
- Indication of the academic level of the work you will carry out (e.g., design, data integration, modelling, assessment) and whether you will work individually or as part of a team
- Location where you will be doing the activities (if partially working from home, please indicate the percentage of time working on location and working from home). You are expected to spend at least 75% of your internship at the location of the organization.
- Time planning: start date + finish date + number of hours per week

## Submission

You need to submit this form at least 2 weeks prior to the start date of the internship, as described on the [course page]({{ site.baseurl }}/) — use your student number in the file name.

You can only start your internship after receiving a GO.
